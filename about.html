Our story

More Than a Bookstore.
A Place for Ideas.

Since 2016, Stackly has helped readers find books worth keeping, from timeless classics to bold new voices.

Since 2016

Books chosen with intention.

Stackly Book Store began in a small Indiranagar shop with a simple belief: finding a meaningful book should feel as memorable as reading one. We connect curious readers with enduring stories, courageous ideas and editions worth keeping.

Every title is considered by readers, for readers, balancing beloved voices with discoveries from beyond the bestseller list.

Our journey

Ten Years of Reading Together

From one small shelf to a community of readers across the country.

2016

The First Shelf

Stackly opens its doors in Indiranagar with a few hundred hand-picked titles.

2018

Online Store

Readers outside the city begin ordering through our website with careful, protective packing.

2021

10,000 Titles

The collection crosses ten thousand books and monthly author evenings begin in store.

2026

A Reader Community

Over 25,000 readers now use wishlists, saved carts and personal dashboards to plan what to read next.

The people behind the shelves

Read by Us. Chosen for You.

Our team includes lifelong booksellers, educators, designers and enthusiastic margin-note makers. Every week, we gather around one table to discuss what deserves a place in the store.

That human conversation, not an algorithm alone, is the heart of our collection.
